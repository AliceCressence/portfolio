About Me

I'm a final year Computer Science student at ICT University, driven by a genuine curiosity for how technology can solve problems that actually affect people. My work spans software development, data science, and embedded systems.

What gets me out of bed is building things with real-world impact — whether that's an emergency blood donation network that could save a life, or a GPS tracking system built from scratch with a microcontroller and a handful of sensors.

I believe the best engineers are the ones who can sit with ambiguity, break down complex problems, and ship solutions that actually work for users — not just on localhost.

Outside of tech, I bring 5+ years of experience as a private tutor for secondary school students — guiding them through Mathematics, Additional Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics, French, and English. I help students work through homework and exercises, build genuine understanding, and walk into exams with confidence. Teaching has made me a sharper thinker and a clearer communicator.
